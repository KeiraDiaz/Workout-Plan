Brisbane · Cycle-Synced

4-Week Calisthenics
Training Plan

Back, upper body & legs. Beginner-friendly. Adjusted for each phase of your menstrual cycle.

About cycle syncing: Research suggests strength and output tend to be highest during the follicular & ovulation phases when oestrogen peaks, and may feel harder during the late luteal phase as progesterone rises and PMS symptoms can appear. This plan adjusts volume and intensity accordingly — not removes training. Every phase still trains. Listen to your body; these are guidelines, not rules.

Follicular (Days 6–12)
